# FurrCircle

Pet care, veterinary management, and trusted local community — Expo / React Native.

> Everything for your pet's care, trusted vets, and local pet people.

- **Pet owners** manage pets, records, medications and vaccines; find and book vets;
  consult by voice or video; and take part in local circles.
- **Vets and clinics** manage availability and appointments, run consultations, keep
  patient records under owner consent, and publish verified community answers.

Start: `npm start`. Architecture and design-system conventions: [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md).
Product specification: [`../FURRCIRCLE_VET_OWNER_PRODUCT_SPEC.md`](../FURRCIRCLE_VET_OWNER_PRODUCT_SPEC.md).
