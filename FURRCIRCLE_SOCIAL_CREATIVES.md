# FurrCircle — Social Media Creatives (Feature-Highlight Series)

Handoff-ready design briefs + copy for Instagram (1080×1080 unless noted).
Drop each brief into Canva, hand to a designer, or paste the "AI image prompt"
into Midjourney / DALL·E / Firefly.

**Core differentiator to express in every visual:** FurrCircle is for *every* pet —
cats, dogs, birds, rabbits, fish, reptiles — not just dogs. Competitors (e.g. doffair)
go dog-only and navy/gold. We go **all-pets** and **bright multi-color**.

---

## Brand kit (use these exact values)

| Token | Hex | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Coral | `#FF6B6B` | Primary accent, key CTAs, highlights |
| Sunshine | `#FFD93D` | Secondary accent, badges, energy pops |
| Pinky | `#FF6FCF` | Tertiary accent, "fun"/social moments |
| Success green | `#4CAF50` | Health/"done"/reminders-complete states |
| Primary blue | `#2563EB` | Links, trust/health features, buttons |
| Surface | `#F7F8FA` | Backgrounds (clean, airy) |
| Foreground | `#1A1A2E` | Headlines & body text (near-black, not pure black) |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, breathing room |

**Typography:** Headlines in a friendly rounded sans (Poppins / Quicksand / Nunito,
bold). Body in a clean sans (Inter / Manrope). Keep it warm, not corporate.

**Motifs:** rounded cards with soft shadows + light glassmorphism (frosted cards on a
soft gradient), multi-species icons (paw + feather + whisker + fish), confetti/heart
sparkles. Always include the FurrCircle logo + 🐾, and one tagline.

**Taglines (rotate):** "Where pet parents belong." · "Connect. Care. Belong." ·
"Your pet's whole world, in one app." · "From first walk to forever."

**Standing CTA options:** "Download FurrCircle — free." · "Create your pet's profile
in under a minute." · "Start your pet's digital passport free."

## Production notes

- Keep a consistent template: logo top-left, tagline in footer, one accent color leading
  per post so the grid still looks varied but unified.
- Rotate the **species shown** across posts (cat, bird, rabbit, fish, reptile, dog) — this
  is the visual proof of "every pet", and it's exactly where dog-only competitors can't follow.
- For carousels, slide 1 = hook headline, last slide = CTA + app store badges + QR code.
- Reuse real in-app screenshots where possible for authenticity (Passport, Match, Feed).
