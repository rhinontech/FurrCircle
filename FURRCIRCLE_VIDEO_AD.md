# FurrCircle — 10-Second Video Ad 🎬

A ready-to-shoot scenario plus copy-paste prompts for AI video generators (Veo 3, Sora, Runway Gen-4, Kling, Pika, Luma).

---

## The concept

**Title:** *"Everything your pet needs. One circle."*

**Emotional hook:** A pet parent's day — chaotic across ten apps — collapses into one warm, joyful app. Fast, bright, heart-tugging, ends on the logo.

**Tone:** Warm, uplifting, modern. Golden-hour light. Indie/desi-friendly setting (Hyderabad parks, apartments) so it feels local and real.

**Music:** Upbeat ukulele/claps building to a single warm chord on the logo. A light "pop" SFX on each app feature that appears.

**Pacing:** 5 micro-scenes × ~2 seconds. Quick, punchy cuts.

## ⭐ Master prompt (single 10s generation)

> Copy-paste this into Veo 3 / Sora / Kling. Best for tools that handle a full 10s arc.

```
A heartwarming 10-second vertical (9:16) advertisement for "FurrCircle," an all-in-one app for pet parents. Cinematic, golden-hour lighting, warm and joyful tone, shallow depth of field, smooth quick cuts.

Scene 1 (0-2s): A cheerful young Indian woman in a sunny park laughs as her brown indie dog joyfully leaps toward her, tongue out, slow-motion. Handheld, intimate.

Scene 2 (2-4s): Close-up of her hands holding a smartphone showing a colorful pet social feed — cute dog and cat photos scrolling, floating heart and paw icons rising from the screen.

Scene 3 (4-6s): The phone screen transitions to a clean health-record interface with checkmarks and a glowing reminder card labeled "Vaccination due."

Scene 4 (6-8s): Quick montage — two happy dogs greet each other on green grass for a playdate; a bright "It's a Match!" card pops up; a map pin drops labeled "Vets nearby."

Scene 5 (8-10s): All visuals gracefully swirl and converge into a glowing paw-print logo on a warm peach-to-coral gradient, the woman hugging her dog softly blurred behind it.

Upbeat ukulele music with claps, gentle pop sound effects. Bright, premium, feel-good mobile-ad aesthetic. No text artifacts.
```

---

## Pro tips for the best result

1. **Generate vertical 9:16** for Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts / TikTok (where pet ads perform best). Make a 16:9 variant for YouTube pre-roll.
2. **Let AI handle motion & emotion, not text** — UI screens and typography look cleaner when overlaid in an editor afterward.
3. **Keep one dog as the "hero"** across shots for continuity (same brown indie dog).
4. **Cut on the beat** — align each ~2s scene change to the music's downbeat.
5. **End card holds 1.5s** so viewers register the logo, tagline, and store badges.
6. Run 3–4 variations of Shot 1 (the hook) — the first 2 seconds decide whether people keep watching.
