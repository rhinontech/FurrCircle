# FurrCircle 🐾
### The all-in-one home for pet parents — social, care, and community in one app.

---

## The one-liner

**FurrCircle is where pet parents connect, care, and belong.** It blends a pet-first social network, a complete digital health record for every pet, and a local community of nearby owners, vets, and rescues — so everything about loving a pet lives in one place.

> *Think Instagram + a pet health passport + a local pet community — built entirely around your animals, not yours.*

---

## The problem we solve

Being a pet parent today means juggling a dozen disconnected tools:

- **Photos and updates** get lost in a general feed full of strangers who don't care about your dog.
- **Vaccination cards, vet bills, and medical history** live in a drawer, a WhatsApp chat, or nowhere at all — and go missing right when the vet asks for them.
- **Finding a playdate, a mate, or an adopter** means scattered Facebook groups and word of mouth.
- **A lost pet** turns into a frantic blast across ten group chats.
- **New pet parents** have no trusted local circle to ask "which vet is good?" or "is this normal?"

There's no single place that understands that your pet is family. **FurrCircle is that place.**

## What's inside FurrCircle

FurrCircle is built around **five pillars** — every one of them already live in the app.

### 1. A social network made for pets 📸

Your pet deserves better than getting buried in a human feed.

- **Smart Feed** — a clean, scrollable feed of posts from people and pets you follow, with a built-in "you're all caught up" boundary and a **Suggested for you** section to discover more once you've seen everything from your circle.
- **Stories** — 24-hour photo & video stories, location-aware so you see what's happening around you.
- **Reels** — full-screen, swipeable video for the most adorable moments.
- **Posts** with photos, videos, captions, locations, likes, comments, saves, and sharing.
- **Rich profiles** for both **owners and pets** — followers, following, bios, and a gallery of every pet in the family.
- **Follow, like, comment, share** — all the social mechanics people already know, tuned for a pet-loving audience.

### 2. Circles — your local pet community 👥

**Circles** are interest- and city-based community groups (Dogs, Cats, Rescue, Health, Training, and more).

- Join circles like *"Hyderabad Indie Dogs & Paws"* or *"Gachibowli Pet Parents Club."*
- **Ask & answer questions** inside each circle — a Q&A knowledge base where real local owners share vet recommendations, training tips, and advice, with upvotes and accepted answers.
- Create your own circle and grow a community around a neighbourhood, breed, or cause.
- Search across **people, pets, posts, circles, questions, and #tags** to find exactly what you need.

### 3. Match — playdates, adoption & breeding 💞

A familiar **swipe-to-match** experience, reimagined for pets, with three modes:

- **🐾 Playdate** — find compatible, nearby pets for a weekend meetup.
- **❤️ Adoption** — discover pets looking for a forever home, send adoption requests, and manage applications (sent & received).
- **🧬 Breed** — responsible, opt-in matching for owners seeking a compatible mate.

Swipe, match, and **chat instantly** when it's mutual — plus a Requests inbox to accept or decline.

### 4. Discover — vets, pets & places near you 🗺️

A location-aware hub that turns "I just moved here" into "I've got this."

- **Nearby vets** — browse, search, and sort vets around you; view profiles, **book appointments**, and read reviews.
- **Pets nearby** — see pets available to **Adopt, Foster, or Breed** in your area.
- **Local places** for everything your pet needs.
- Auto-detects your city so the right results show up immediately.

### 5. Pet health & care — a digital pet passport 🩺

This is where FurrCircle becomes indispensable. Every pet gets a complete, always-with-you health record:

- **Medical Records / Pet Passport** — vaccination history, allergies, medications, vitals, insurance, and microchip details, all in one place.
- **Easy logging** — record a **vaccine**, **vitals** (temperature, heart rate, weight, blood pressure), or **medications** in seconds.
- **Care reminders** — never miss a deworming, booster, or monthly checkup; smart reminders surface what's due.
- **Care services & vet booking** — book checkups right when a reminder says it's time.
- **The "Today" hub** — a daily home base for reminders, schedules, upcoming birthdays, and playdate suggestions.

## And the moments that make it special ✨

- **🆘 Lost & Found** — report a lost pet (or a spotted/found one) with a photo and details; the whole community can help bring them home, with live status tracking.

- **🎂 Birthdays** — never miss a gotcha-day or birthday; the app counts down and celebrates with you.
- **💬 Chat** — direct messaging between owners, with rich shares (posts, profiles, circles) and match conversations built in.
