## Part 4 — How to NOT get banned or flagged 🚫

1. **Read each group's rules** — look for "no self-promo," "promo day only," or "admin approval required."
2. **Comment value first.** A helpful answer + one soft mention beats a bare link. Bare links get deleted.
3. **Don't paste identical text.** Facebook's spam filter flags repeated identical comments/links across groups. Rotate the variations above and tweak a few words each time.
4. **Space it out.** Don't post to 20 groups in 10 minutes — that looks like a bot. A handful per day, spread out.
5. **Use your real personal profile** that has activity, a photo, and friends — fresh/empty accounts get auto-flagged.
6. **Engage genuinely** — like and reply to a few posts before/around your promo. Pure broadcasting gets noticed.
7. **Prefer native engagement over links** when you can — Facebook throttles reach on posts with external links. Sometimes "search FurrCircle on the App Store / Play Store" outperforms a raw link.
8. **Localize** — swap in the city name ([CITY]) so it feels relevant, not mass-blasted.
9. **Ask admins** — a quick DM asking "may I share a free pet app I built?" often gets a yes and a pinned spot.
10. **Track what works** — note which template + group brings installs, and double down there.
